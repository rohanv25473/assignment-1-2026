# Identify aspiration through expressed desire to buy or own

Status: accepted, Q33-Q38. Scope: Task F.

Count the author's expressed desire to buy or own a marque, including clear conditional or dream ownership. Keep concrete purchase intent and conditional desire as separate categories. Restricting the analysis to concrete purchase plans was considered but would omit genuine ownership wishes. Praise alone, current ownership alone, recommendations to others, negation, and quoted-only desire do not qualify.

Evaluate all reliably identified marques discovered in Task A, rather than restricting to its top 10, and show supporting counts for sparse brands. The result describes expressed desire in the corpus; dream ownership must not be presented as an imminent sale. The definition requires semantic evidence rather than one aspiration keyword.

Rank by distinct authors expressing either qualifying desire category, counting each author once per marque. Show separate concrete and conditional rankings, post counts, and the fraction of each marque's discussing authors who express desire. Post-count ranking would allow one frequent poster to dominate; a normalized fraction alone could exaggerate a tiny audience. Authors may appear for multiple marques; the ranking is not a mutually exclusive market-share estimate.

Keep GM-only desire at parent level unless a child marque or clear model is identified, while preserving GM expansion for earlier mention statistics. This deliberately differs from inherited attribute links in E: desire for a corporate family does not establish desire for each child. The user accepted this after discussing false specificity, multiplication of one desire across four rankings, and weakened managerial conclusions.

Count qualifying desire expressed at any point in the corpus period, once per author-brand, even if the author later rejects the marque. Flag conflicting evidence. Inferring a latest stance was rejected because chronology is coarse; period-level expressed desire must not be labeled current intent.

Review aspiration in the existing audit, plus up to 10 predicted aspiration posts and 10 difficult nonpositive/uncertain posts, reusing reviewed posts where possible. Include leading brands and boundary cases involving negation, conditional wishes, ownership, and quotes. These targeted diagnostics cannot establish unbiased accuracy or recall.
