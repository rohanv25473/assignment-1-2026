# Tie client recommendations to evidence and sensitivity

Status: accepted, Q39-Q40. Scope: Task G.

Present about three actionable recommendations for JD Power and its clients. Each should name the action, supporting findings, a limitation that could change the advice, and a way to test its value. Let the evidence determine the recommendations rather than requiring one recommendation from each analysis or substituting a general insight summary.

Label a conclusion robust when relevant sensitivity checks and supporting evidence sustain it; label method-dependent conclusions explicitly and explain what would change them. The historical corpus can inform benchmarking and research priorities, but it cannot establish current market preferences or causal effects on sales. No specific recommendations can be finalized before the analyses exist.
